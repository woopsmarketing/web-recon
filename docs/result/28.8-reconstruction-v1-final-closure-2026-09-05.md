## Executive Summary

**Final verdict: `NOT READY - GENERIC PRODUCT BLOCKERS REMAIN`.** Chosen by an independent adjudicator
that implemented none of the work and was given no preferred answer.

Phase A ran in full. Its seven-clause hard gate **failed**: three clauses NOT MET (B width, C protect,
G visual), one MET (D popup), three PARTIAL (A blank content, E QA honesty, F calibration). Both
permitted correction cycles were spent. Per §31 and §32, **Phase B did not start** — the four fresh
public sites (channel.io, toss.im, vipgunma.com, beomeo.roseeskin.com) were preflighted only.

What genuinely closed this wave: two whole defect classes that had **no QA channel at all** in 28.7 now
have one; the severance BLOCKERs are gone (`overlap-excess-ratio` 0.4484 → 0.0016 @1100, 0.3425 → 0.0012
@1440); the desktop popup gate (28.7's B6) is closed on live evidence at both 390 and 1440; and the
regression grew from 4,326 to **4,663 checks with 0 failures**.

What did not close is the thing the product is judged on. The reconstruction still keeps a **frozen
desktop canvas at intermediate widths**, and the independent visual auditor graded **0 of 7** pairs
acceptable at 700/1024/1100 against **4 of 5** at 1440. 28.7 pre-registered the acceptance test for this
wave — `footer-clipped = 0` on linear `/` at 700, 1024, 1100 — and the final run reports **1 at all
three**. The adjudicator confirmed the same mechanism by eye on a legacy Korean hospital CMS that shares
no code path or vendor with linear.app, which is what makes it generic rather than a site quirk.

Human-acceptable share of the corpus moved from roughly 20% to **39%** (7 of 18 pairs). Real, measured,
and not enough.

## Phase A Changes

Five lanes plus two correction cycles and four regression repairs. Reports: `01-` … `08-` under
`docs/result/28.75/`.

### Blank Region Detection

New `blank-region-ratio` channel, rubric 4 → 5. A region census (≥20% viewport-wide, ≥60px tall, ≥2%
area) examined 32,652 candidates and selected 790 against ten enumerated rejection reasons, so
intentional whitespace is never called a defect. Two independent evidence legs — DOM paint occupancy and
region-level screenshot ink — are ORed and unioned. Correspondence is by tag-path **suffix**, because
exact path matching scored 0%: the generator bolts on a fixed shell prefix.

It fires where it must (BLOCKER 1.4533 on the pre-fix severance build, naming hero / NEWS / promo
carousel) and stays silent on the linear `/pricing` must-not-fire controls. Suite 205 → 271.

**It also produced a false positive on its first exposure to a page outside its fixture set.** On
seoultone @1440 it fired BLOCKER 0.5767; the independent visual auditor found the doctor biography
*fully present* — portrait, 김진용 대표원장 plate, education and appointment columns — and **more
complete than the source**, which drops the entire 병원위치/주차안내/진료시간 column and all five 약속
bullets. What it flagged is a 183px grey strip below the certificate carousel: intentional section
padding roughly 88px too tall. §9 required that this channel never classify intentional whitespace as a
defect. On a fifth site, it did.

**Named hole:** the ink leg is withheld on every hobbang pair (DOM census 47–55% against an 0.8 floor).
The trigger is page length, so long pages systematically lose paint-occlusion detection. Disclosed
in-artifact, not surfaced in `responsive-qa.json`.

### JS-Dependent Content Recovery

28.7's B2 assumed a JavaScript runtime would be needed. It was not — all three regions were static
observation defects, and **no runtime emulator was built**:

| region | category | owner | mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| severance hero | C | reconstruction | the observed `<body>` background was emitted as an in-flow block background instead of propagating to the document canvas, painting over a `z-index:-1` hero and leaving white text on white |
| severance NEWS | C | observer | `float` was absent from the computed-style whitelist, so slick's `float:left` slides stacked at the track origin (x = −543 under `translateX(-981px)`) and were clipped by `.slick-list{overflow:hidden}` |
| severance promo carousel | C | observer | the same missing `float:left` on a second track (`translateX(-777px)`) |
| seoultone doctor bio | A | observer | `html{scroll-behavior:smooth}` makes `window.scrollBy` asynchronous; `runScrollPass` read `window.scrollY` synchronously and broke on `moved<=0` after travelling 0px, so ScrollReveal never fired below the fold |

Result: severance's two 28.7 BLOCKERs are closed — `overlap-excess-ratio` 0.4484 → **0.0016** @1100 and
0.3425 → **0.0012** @1440, confirmed by eye (the NEWS row carries all cards where it had none).

### Frozen Width Chain Root

6,927 residuals resolved to 1,107 chain roots. Three mechanisms — `trackedFillWidth()`,
`viewportBleedWidth()`, `damageClampWidth()` — under a strict four-part predicate, plus
`recoverGridTracksBanded()` and `gridAreaFillWidth()` from correction cycle 1. The work is
**refusal-dominated**: 150 clamps against 7,194 refusals, each with a stated reason.

Two-directional geometry over 190,590 comparisons: 2,810 improved, 37 worsened, Σ width error
−5.85%. Correction cycle 1 added hobbang width 4,922 improved / 12 worsened (−36.9%) and x 2,985 / 110
(−58.6%).

A discriminating twin caught a real regression before it shipped: the damage clamp compared against the
parent's **border** box while `100%` names its **content** box (+62.0px on `n000575` @1280, +307.9px on
its child @1101). Fixed with `containingBlockAt()`.

**It did not cure the product defect.** See Generic Blockers.

### Popup Consistency

A two-tier additive gate, viewport-**relative** throughout:

    admitted  ⇔ painting ∧ position ∈ {fixed, absolute} ∧ intersects viewport ∧ (COVER ∨ PANEL)
    PANEL     ⇔ ¬(w ≥ .85 ∧ h < .5) ∧ w ≥ .20 ∧ h ≥ .25 ∧ area ≥ .08
                ∧ (min(insetL,insetR) ≥ .06 ∨ min(insetT,insetB) ≥ .06) ∧ (fixed ∨ z-index > 0)
    qualified ⇔ admitted ∧ strongSignals ≥ 1 ∧ signals ≥ 2      (UNCHANGED)

Zero false positives across 56 page×viewport combinations. On the live seoultone origin the final run
records `qualified=1 dismissed=1` at **both** 390 and 1440 — closing B6 at the width where the old
absolute shape test was blind. **Known residual:** PANEL's 0.20 width floor re-blinds above ~2500px.

### QA L1/L3 Closure

**B7** closed: one shared page-state policy with an explicit two-call contract on both the observer and
QA-source sides (`markInitialPaintCensus` then `normalizePageState`), with `SideProvenance.pageState` and
ineligible-with-reason.

**L1** closed: geometry and ownership run first, and a demoted overlap can still reach the verdict
through a new `overlap-demoted-excess-ratio` channel measured against the source's own demoted area.

**L3** closed in responsive-QA: identical `gatePixels` numbers plus 19 excess-band fields with an
asserted conservation identity, a red `CAPTURE WIDTH MISMATCH` banner and a hatched uncompared strip.
It fired for real on linear `/` @700 — 700px source against an 862px clone, the 162px band **99.98%
ink**, only **78% of the capture ever compared**. Rubric 5 → 6.

**Not closed:** `src/reconstruction-qa/screenshot-diff.ts:209` still performs the same silent min-crop on
a second code path. Documented, and carrying a check that fails the day it is fixed.

## Generic Blockers

Ordered by cost to the product. These are the five to fix.

### 1. Frozen desktop canvas at intermediate widths
linear `/` and `/pricing` at 700/1024/1100, severance at 1100. The page lays out wider than the viewport
and is **clipped, not scrolled** (`scrollWidth` equals the viewport), and because the frozen layout is
centred it is clipped on **both** sides. Nav links drop off one at a time — at 700 the header has no
controls at all; footer Connect/Legal columns become unreachable; body copy is cut mid-sentence;
unfittable flex children collapse into half-page voids. Correction cycle 2 released exactly one width
(@700: 1838 → 1575 against a source at 1627). It could not release 1024/1100: the desktop probe was
already at 2291/2306, so those widths were never starved of measurement — they are **refused** by the
containing-block guards.

### 2. CJK line-box collision — now the most widespread defect class
The independent auditor broadened this class after seeing seoultone, and it is the one class that shows
up on **every Korean-language site in the corpus**. hobbang `/` at 390/1100/1440: footer labels wrap
mid-word and their tails collide with the row beneath, illegible at 390. seoultone `/` at both widths:
in 진료시간 **every closing time overprints the next row's label, so the opening hours cannot be read**,
plus a tagline over the DERMATOLOGY wordmark and 대표원장 over a credential line. Proven **not** a
grid-track problem — hobbang's footer pixels are byte-identical across the pre- and post-cycle-1
engines. Unreadable business-critical information (opening hours) is a stronger product failure than the
`overlap-excess-ratio` numbers (0.3173 / 0.1827) suggest.

### 3. Only two DOM trees exist, and it is now load-bearing
Mobile (390) and desktop (1440), switched between. At 700 the source serves a third layout neither tree
contains. While the clone was frozen this was invisible — it *over*-rendered, 8,835 chars against a
source's 7,450. Now that it honestly reflows, `missing-text-ratio` at 700 is 0.157. §6 listed this as
carry-don't-block, which was right while the freeze hid it. It no longer does.

### 4. The rubric's top band is miscalibrated in both directions
*Too strict:* 0 of 18 machine PASS while humans pass two; `pixel-residual-difference-ratio` fires on
every pair, best value 0.0152 against its own 0.01 threshold — on a pair two independent observers call
PASS. *Wrong unit:* `missing-text-ratio` counts an animation re-frame as content loss. *Too lenient:*
hobbang `/` @390 is machine MINOR and human BLOCKER, because an 80px footer collision averaged over a
17,167px page is invisible to a whole-page pixel ratio. *And it mis-fires:* `blank-region-ratio` called
BLOCKER on seoultone @1440 for intentional padding, on a page whose content is present and richer than
the source's. The instrument over-calls, under-calls and false-positives, so its verdict distribution is
not a product score. Machine and human disagreed on **10 of 18 pairs**, in both directions.

### 5. Content-node loss and asset corruption
linear `/pricing` @1024 drops all seven customer logos, leaving a blank band; hobbang silently drops one
of eleven verification-table rows at 1100 and 1440; severance renders six quick-menu icons as
disconnected arcs.

## What We Refused To Build

- **No React/Vue runtime emulator.** B2 was assumed to need one; it did not, and all three regions were
  fixed as static observation defects.
- **No transitive descendant unfreeze** — already disproven in 28.7 and not retried.
- **No leaf-rule accumulation** — the width work attacks chain roots, and refuses 7,194 times.
- **No blanket `max-width:100%`**, which would have moved the numbers and broken intentional full-bleed.
- **No third DOM tree**, which was out of scope and is now a named blocker instead of a silent fix.
- **No assertion weakened to make a suite pass**, in four separate regression repairs.
- **No Phase B**, because the gate failed.

## Why Reconstruction Development Should Stop Here

Not because the system is finished — it is not — but because continuing would not be measuring anything
new. Three reasons:

1. **The dominant blocker is fully understood and does not need more research.** Its mechanism, its
   population, its refusal reasons and its exact per-width numbers are all recorded. It needs a design
   decision about intermediate-width layout (a third tree, or genuinely reflowable emission), not another
   investigation.
2. **The instrument is now the binding constraint on judgement.** With 0 machine PASS and two-directional
   human disagreement, more automated runs would produce more numbers of unknown calibration.
3. **The next honest step needs a human.** Whether 39% human-acceptable with a known, named, single
   dominant defect class is worth continuing is a product decision, not a technical one.
