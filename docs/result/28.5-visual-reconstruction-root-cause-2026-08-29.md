# Task 28.5A — Visual Reconstruction Root-Cause Investigation

**Subject:** raw Exact Reconstruction of linear.app, accepted lineage
`data/linear.app/reconstructions/2026-08-25T20-30-33-313Z/app`
**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Waves:** 1 (map + audit) → 2 (root causes + experiments) → 3 (independent rechecks + adjudication)
**Status:** forensic audit. **No fixes are applied by this document.**

**Measurement caveat that governs every number below.** The pipeline's headline `changedPixelRatio` counts a
pixel as changed when any channel differs by ≥1/255 (`src/reconstruction-qa/screenshot-diff.ts:24-31`). Its
perceptible fraction ranges from 26% to 98% depending on route, so it is **not comparable route-to-route**.
Every ratio in this report is written with its threshold (`@1` = ≥1/255, `@16` = ≥16/255, `dE76` = CIELab).
A ratio without a threshold is a defect in this report, not a finding.

**Source-truth policy.** The 2026-08-25 observation is historical truth. linear.app has changed since; anything
captured on 2026-08-28/29 is labelled CURRENT_SOURCE and is never historical truth.

## 1. Executive Summary

**The raw Exact Reconstruction is visibly wrong before any authoring happens, and the entire measured delta
belongs to the raw reconstruction artifact — nothing downstream of it creates any.** 17 of 19 audited
route/viewport pairs are "obviously different", 1 is "unusable", 1 is "minor"
(`docs/result/handoffs/28.5-raw-visual-audit.json` → `verdictTally`). The recon-template boundary is
MD5-identical on 8/8 route/viewport pairs with `reconstruction-data` byte-identical
(`docs/result/28.5-visual-fidelity-evidence/stage5-template-vs-recon-pixel-diff.json`), so raw reconstruction
carries 100% of the delta.

*Stated that way deliberately, because three claims are easy to merge and only two of them are supported.*
§10's **earliest loss point** column assigns RC-01, RC-02, RC-03, RC-04, RC-12 and RC-13 to **OBSERVATION**,
not to the generation stage defined in §2(3). So: **observation** is where most of the information is
destroyed; **generation** is the first stage whose *output* can render differently from the source (§2, §7);
and the **raw reconstruction artifact** is what carries the entire measured delta (§8). "The delta is created
inside generation" would be false. This report keeps the three apart everywhere.

**The failure is one mechanism repeated.** 17 of 31 catalogued defects are the same shape: a closed,
hand-curated allowlist drops a property, and the browser then silently substitutes that property's **CSS
initial value** — `opacity:1` for a suppressed pseudo, `-webkit-font-smoothing:auto`, `fill:black`,
`mask-composite:add`, and no `@font-face` at all. `ALLOWED_CSS_PROPERTIES` is literally
`STYLE_WHITELIST + PSEUDO_STYLE_WHITELIST` (`src/reconstruction/style-generator.ts:39-43`), so a property the
Observer never captured is structurally unemittable.

**The five findings that matter tomorrow.**

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Zero font faces ship | `@font-face` **0** in an 11,267,135-byte stylesheet naming `Inter Variable` **4,188** times; **0** font files on disk; `document.fonts.size = 0` | 8/8 routes, 19/19 pairs |
| 2 | Responsive rules are effectively absent | `scrollHeight` **bit-identical** 390→914px on 4/4 routes swept; **507** visible nodes vanish on /pricing in 1024–1231.98px; all **710** `@media` blocks target the desktop subtree | contributes **0.0000** to every published number |
| 3 | Pseudo-elements paint suppressed states | **114** `::before` bars painting at `opacity:1`; **56** of them recorded in the observation with 18-property tokens carrying neither `opacity` nor `visibility` | 91.6% of /pricing desktop **@1**; ~0% **@16**; 93.9% at dE76≥2.3 |
| 4 | `layout-inference` regresses its own truth viewport | **349** of 1,910 recovered-rule nodes render *farther* from their observed box, **0** closer, **98,613px** of introduced width error; /changelog prose column 624px → **303.27px** | 1440 truth viewport |
| 5 | Pixel fidelity gates nothing | `screenshot-diff.ts:29` — "No PASS score (item 31). Nothing here returns a verdict." `changedPixelRatio` has **zero** consumers outside `src/reconstruction-qa/` | whole pipeline |

**Why QA passed anyway.** `QA_STYLE_PROPERTIES = STYLE_WHITELIST` (`src/reconstruction-qa/capture-page.ts:46`):
the verifier's comparison vocabulary *is* the loss channel. `grep -rln pseudo src/reconstruction-qa/` returns

signals report healthy on pages that loaded zero faces. The accepted run reported
`screenshotChangedRatioMedian 0.1679` (@1) and `assetFailures 202` beside `routeCheck {checked 8, rendered 8,

**Verdict.** RAW RECONSTRUCTION NEEDS TARGETED FIDELITY HARDENING — with two named weaknesses stated in §28:
the largest perceptible win (fonts) is licensing-gated, not engineering-gated; and the responsive fix is the
one item classified REPLACE SUBSYSTEM inside a hardening verdict, with a benefit that is honestly
**unmeasured in the recommended form**.

## 2. How Reconstruction Actually Works Today

**Observe → IR → regenerate.** No source CSS text ever survives; the whole style layer is re-derived from a
closed list of computed values.

**(1) Observer.** `src/observer/observe-page.ts:209` renders one URL per viewport profile in Chromium and, per
element, records:

- a closed **`STYLE_WHITELIST` of 111 computed properties** (`src/observer/types.ts:260-388`);
- a smaller **18-property `PSEUDO_STYLE_WHITELIST`** for `::before`/`::after`, recorded only when computed
  `content` is renderable (`src/observer/types.ts:484-519`);
- an `ATTR_WHITELIST` of 25 names plus every `aria-*`/`data-*`;
- a bounded sample of **authored** matched declarations — `LAYOUT_RULE_PROPERTIES` = 62 layout-only
  properties, capped at `MAX_LAYOUT_RULES 2000` per page and `MAX_MATCHED_RULES_PER_ELEMENT 32`
  (`src/observer/collect-dom.ts:243-362`);
- an optional multi-width **layout probe** at `[390, 768, 1024, 1440, 1920]` recording **only** `rect.x`,
  `rect.width` and a visibility bit (`src/observer/layout-probe.ts:125-158`).

`SKIP_TAGS` (`src/observer/types.ts:204-214`, 9 tags including `HEAD`, `STYLE`, `LINK`) drops those elements
and their subtrees, so **no source stylesheet text is ever carried forward**. Styles are deduplicated into a
shared style table (`styles.json`, one `styleId` per node) by exact equality on a sorted JSON key — provably
lossless *within the allowlist* (`src/sitespec/style-catalog.ts:55-58`). Context is hard-pinned: locale
`ko-KR`, timezone `Asia/Seoul`, `colorScheme: 'light'`, `reducedMotion: 'no-preference'`
(`src/observer/types.ts:152-155`).

**(2) SiteSpec.** An IR (`site-spec.json`, `style-catalog.json` 17.4MB / 4,452 tokens, `asset-catalog.json`
439 assets, 18,918 element nodes, 7,794 text nodes). **It is never rendered**, so it cannot itself produce a
pixel difference.

**(3) Reconstruction generation — the first stage whose output can render differently from the source.**
`src/reconstruction/style-generator.ts` emits **one CSS class per style token**
(`st000123` → `.wr-st000123 { … }`) carrying every observed property.
`ALLOWED_CSS_PROPERTIES = STYLE_WHITELIST + PSEUDO_STYLE_WHITELIST` (`style-generator.ts:39-43`) — this single
line is the causal chain for most of this report. A **second, RECOVERED-LAYOUT tier** from
`src/reconstruction/layout-inference.ts` re-derives centred-max-width / full-width / percentage-width /
responsive-hidden rules from the probe and emits them at selector specificity **(0,3,0)**
(`layout-inference.ts:468-472`), which **outranks** the exact `.wr-stNNNNNN` class at (0,1,0). Both viewport
variant trees are mounted in **one document**, gated by `data-wr-viewport`. Output is a prebuilt Next 16 app.

Shipped artifact, verified: `app/public/wr/generated-styles.css` is **11,267,135 bytes / 13,962 lines**, with
the recovered tier appended as one contiguous block at **line 6686** carrying exactly **1,910 rules** —
1,148 `width:auto` (1,106 full-width + 42 centred), 52 percentage-width, 710 responsive-hidden.

**(4) Recon template.** Adds 3,079 slots / 9,929 bindings on top and is **provably paint-neutral**: 8/8
route/viewport pairs MD5-identical, `changedRatio 0`, `meanDelta 0`, `reconstruction-data` byte-identical,
exactly 3 app source files differing (`stage5-template-vs-recon-pixel-diff.json`). An independent width sweep
reproduced its document heights exactly (/ @1440 = 10898, / @390 = 6382, /pricing @1440 = 6348,
/pricing @390 = 7712).

## 6. What Is Discarded

Everything below is dropped with **no counter, no flag and no artifact field** unless stated.

- **All source stylesheets.** `STYLE` and `LINK` are in `SKIP_TAGS` (`types.ts:204-214`). The two channels
  that could still have recovered rule text both skip cross-origin sheets silently: the **authored-rule
  harvest** builds its index from `document.styleSheets` and drops any sheet that throws on `.cssRules`
  (`collect-dom.ts:243-362`, declared in its own comment at `:246-248`), and the **font-URL harvest** at
  `:745-772` swallows the identical throw in a bare `catch { continue; }` at **`:749-751`**. 100% of
  linear.app's CSS is cross-origin (28 sheets on /security, 30 on /pricing throw `SecurityError` on
  `.cssRules`).
- **All CSS custom properties.** `collect-dom.ts:394-403` iterates a closed allowlist; the `style` attribute is
  not in `ATTR_WHITELIST` and is dropped again at `safe-attributes.ts:131-137`. Result: **0** `--x:`
  declarations in 11.27MB of output, against **779** surviving `fill="var(--…)"` attributes that now resolve
  to nothing, and 613 custom properties defined by the source.
- **All `@font-face` rules and all font files.** 0 of each; `asset-catalog.json kindCounts` has no `font` key
  on any of 8 pages.
- **All `@keyframes`**, against 26 emitted `animation-name` declarations naming 9 keyframe sets.
- **SVG interiors.** The DOM walk `return`s at `<svg>` (`collect-dom.ts:665-671`); no `<path>`/`<g>`/`<stop>`
  ever receives a `styleId` or a box, and `fill`/`stroke`/`stroke-width`/`stop-color` are in no whitelist.
- **Shadow roots, iframes, canvas pixels, video frames.** Hosts are inventoried
  (`collect-dom.ts:653-658`), never entered (`:673-675`). `adoptedStyleSheets` is referenced **nowhere** in
  `src/` — and linear.app ships one 3,460-byte constructed sheet defining the `--sx-*` palette on both audited
  routes, invisible to `outerHTML` *and* to `document.styleSheets`.
- **59.6% of observed attribute occurrences** (19,773 of 33,191) at `safe-attributes.ts:131-137`, including
  ~2,300 occurrences of live visual state (`data-state` 276, `data-loaded` 290, `data-theme` 100…). No
  limitation code covers this.
- **Vertical responsive behaviour.** The probe records only `x`, `width`, visibility — `y` and `height` at
  probe widths are unknowable by construction (`layout-probe.ts:125-158`).
- **Dark mode and reduced-motion variants**, by hard-pinned context (`types.ts:152-155`).
- **`srcset`/`sizes`**, dead before the template boundary (`asset-resolver.ts:70-99` + `ATTR_MAX_LEN 500`), so
  every image renders its 1× `src` even on the DSF3 mobile capture.
- **Element text beyond `TEXT_MAX_LEN 200`** with no per-element flag (98 nodes sit at exactly 200 chars).

## 8. Raw Source vs Exact Reconstruction

**Yes — the raw reconstruction is visually wrong, decisively, before any authoring.** This was confirmed by
eye on band composites, not only by number.

- **/pricing desktop** (`28.5-visual-fidelity-evidence/r000007/desktop/bands/band-02-cr76.png`, a
  source|clone|diff triptych): the plan comparison table renders its ~252 check/cross glyphs as near-invisible
  dark discs on a dark ground (contrast **13.64:1 → 1.15:1**), and **every** table row carries a permanently
  painted hover bar the source suppresses. The table conveys no plan information at all.
- **/changelog desktop** (`r000002/desktop/bands/band-05-cr25.png`): the entry date column is halved, so
  "July 30, 2026" stacks over three lines; a neighbouring label collapses to **one character per line** running
  vertically; the body column narrows enough to re-wrap the heading; the embedded video is an empty black box.

By the product definition of fidelity that fails on colour, contrast, line wrapping, crop and content presence
simultaneously.

**And the delta belongs entirely to raw reconstruction.** SiteSpec is an IR and is never rendered; the
recon-template boundary is pixel- and byte-identical on 8/8 pairs. So "the problem is downstream" is
eliminated by artifact, not by argument.

Accepted QA headline for the same artifact: `screenshotChangedRatioMedian` **0.1679 @1** over 16 pairs,
`screenshotMeanDeltaMedian` 7.6295, `assetFailures` 202, `unstablePages` 5 — recorded, and gating nothing.

## 11. Typography / Font Findings

**Fonts are the broadest cause in the corpus — present on 8/8 routes and 19/19 pairs — and the largest at a
perceptible threshold on text-dominated routes.**

**Structural facts, independently re-verified three times.** In the 11,267,135-byte
`app/public/wr/generated-styles.css`: `@font-face` **0**, `-webkit-font-smoothing` **0**,
`font-feature-settings` **0**, `text-rendering` **0**, `.woff2` **0**, `@keyframes` **0**. `find` for
woff/woff2/ttf/otf under the app: **0 files**. Against that: `"Inter Variable"` **4,188** references,
`Berkeley Mono` **109**. Live clone probes: `document.fonts.size = 0` on /pricing and /changelog;
`getComputedStyle().webkitFontSmoothing === 'auto'` (the CSS initial value) on **5,971/5,971** and
**6,569/6,569** elements respectively — where linear.app sets `antialiased`. The live source loads **3** faces
in all 16 source captures.

**Magnitude, from a controlled 2×2 ladder rebuilt from scratch on 7 desktop routes** (A = base, B = smoothing
only, C = faces only, D = both; identical injection point, identical waits, identical context):

| Route | faces alone (A→C) @1 | smoothing alone (A→B) @1 | both (A→D) @1 | both @16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| /security | 27.7% | 5.0% | 80.4% | **78.5%** |
| /integrations | 26.1% | 4.3% | 85.8% | **97.4%** |
| /customers/automattic | 29.6% | 2.1% | 79.5% | **79.4%** |
| /pricing | — | — | — | 74.5% |
| / (home) | — | — | — | 46.6% |
| /changelog | — | — | — | 16.9% |
| /plan | — | — | — | **5.8%** |

Four things must travel with those numbers.

1. **It is an AND-gate, not two independent multipliers.** Smoothing alone buys 0.9–5.0%. Correcting
   rasterization on the wrong outlines is nearly worthless. Ship faces first.
2. **"Co-equal" is wrong.** Under a symmetric (Shapley) split of the joint effect, faces carry
   **1.7–2.3×** smoothing's weight on `@16` pixel counts and **4.7–5.4×** on error energy (meanΔ).
3. **Super-additivity is real, not a composition artifact.** Excess of "both" over the additive sum was
   positive on **7 of 7** routes at `@1`, `@16` and meanΔ alike (+41.4 to +55.8 pp at `@16`).
4. **`-webkit-font-smoothing` is a macOS-only knob.** Every number here was measured on darwin 25.2.0. On a
   Linux or Windows CI capture host this half would measure near zero. Do not size the fix from these figures
   for a CI-hosted observer.

**Mechanism, measured rather than asserted.** Going C→D (correct outlines, AA off → on) strips **15–20
percentage points** of excess glyph coverage while horizontal glyph extents barely move (row-extent match
within 1px goes 70.4→73.1%, 90.1→93.0%, 83.8→86.5%). At rung A (substitution) extent match is only
20.1/45.2/13.0%. "More ink at identical advance" is exactly what smoothing does and exactly what substitution
cannot fake. Wave 1's "+25–42% more ink" magnitude does **not** reproduce — the correct figure is +12 to +19%,
and wave 2's own artifact already filed that claim as refuted.

**Two residual typography defects that survive a perfect font fix.** `text-wrap-mode`/`text-wrap-style`
(RC-13) — `white-space` is already at `types.ts:335` and is not sufficient; adding the two removes **50.4%** of
the /security residual after fonts (C5 0.007372 → C6 0.003660 `@1`, reproduced bit-identically twice), and
four elements produce all of it. And shaping/variation-axis properties (RC-14): rung D → E takes
/customers/automattic 0.01335 → 0.00705 `@1`, a further 47% of what survives.

**The health signals are structurally unfireable.** `document.fonts.ready` resolves instantly when there are
no faces (`capture-page.ts:505-521`, `fontsReadyMs` 2–3ms); `document.fonts.check()` returned **true** both
with 0 faces and with 4; the font-family-mismatch classifier (`types.ts:342`) compares the declared family
*string*, which is byte-identical under total substitution. `final-summary.json` therefore reports
`requiresFontBindingObservation: 0` in all 12 classifications, on a lineage where zero faces loaded on 8/8
routes.

## 12. CSS / Responsive Findings

**This is the defect most underweighted by the audit's own metrics: it contributes exactly 0.0000 to every
published number and is the worst failure in the corpus by the product definition of fidelity.**

**Defect 1 — no reflow at all below the breakpoint.** An 11-width × 4-route sweep and a 16-width × 8-route
sweep, both on independently served instances, agree: `scrollHeight` is **bit-identical** to its 390px value at
every width from 391 to 914 on **every route tested**, and jumps only at 915.

| Route | 390 | 500 | 700 | 914 | 915 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| / | 6382 | 6382 | 6382 | 6382 | → desktop tree |
| /pricing | 7712 | 7712 | 7712 | 7712 | → 6348 |
| /security | 4783 | 4783 | 4783 | 4783 | → 3231 |
| /changelog | 24482 | 24482 | 24482 | 24482 | → desktop tree |

Zero reflow across a **524px range**. Mechanism, read out of the code and confirmed by census: **all 710**
`@media` blocks in the shipped stylesheet carry `data-wr-viewport="desktop"` in their selector, because
`generateLayoutCss` hardcodes that attribute (`layout-inference.ts:470`). **The mobile subtree has literally
zero responsive rules of any kind.**

**Defect 2 — the synthetic band deletes content.** Visible-element counts drop inside 1024–1231.98px on
**8 of 8** routes and restore at 1232: /pricing **1702 → 1023** desktop-variant nodes (507 of 1,371 go
`display:none`, including the entire `Features|Free|Basic|Business|Enterprise` header row at 1344×56 and every
Basic/Business/Enterprise cell), /plan 673 → 621, /integrations 1197 → 1127, /customers 1172 → 1120,
/changelog 2004 → 1992, / 2435 → 2428, /automattic 241 → 233, /security 386 → 378. `scrollHeight` stays pinned
at 6348 on /pricing from 915 through 1231, so the holes are dead space. The live source restores the full
table by 1100, so the clone is *also* wrong in the other direction for ~132px.

**Why exactly two bands exist — forced arithmetic, not a stylesheet coincidence.** Probe widths are
`[390,768,1024,1440,1920]`; `layout-inference.ts:131` filters to `width >= breakpoint(915)`, leaving
`{1024,1440,1920}`; `hiddenRanges()` (`:426-455`) midpoints those three. Exactly **709** blocks at
`(min-width:1024px) and (max-width:1231.98px)` plus **1** at `(min-width:1680px)`, and **0** `@container`
rules, are the only conditions this design can express *for any site*. The same line **discards the fully
populated 768px probe row** (1,371 nodes, 792 visible on p000007) — the observation already holds evidence
that would partly mitigate defect 1.

**The `layout-inference` denominator bug (RC-05).** `contentAt(i) = parentBorderBoxWidth −
parentHorizontalPadding` (`layout-inference.ts:205-211`) is only valid when the node's containing block **is**
the parent's content box. It is wrong for grid items (containing block is the grid **area**), for flex items
sized by basis/grow, for absolutely positioned nodes (nearest positioned ancestor's **padding** box), and for
any parent with a border (padding is subtracted, border-width never is).

Worked arithmetic, reproduced in the browser: parent `n000663` is a 12-column grid, border-box width 1280 at
both 1440 and 1920, zero horizontal padding. Child `n000668` probe widths `[342,375,460,624,624]`. Ratios
against `contentAt` at `{1024,1440,1920}` are 0.4832/0.4875/0.4875, mean **0.48606** → emitted `width: 48.61%`.
CSS then resolves that percentage against the item's **624px grid area**, rendering **303.27px**. Exactly 10
nodes on p000002 carry the 48.61%/624px pair — all 10 changelog entries — plus 10 date columns at 22.91%
(296 → 67.80px). The accepted QA's own geometry block for that page records width **p90 = p95 = 320.73**
(= 624 − 303.27) across 935 nodes with **571 mismatched**, and the run recorded `status: "complete"`.

**The module's docstring guarantee is false, and structurally unenforced.** `layout-inference.ts:19-22` claims
"every emitted rule must REPRODUCE the observed truth-viewport geometry, so the 1440px rendering cannot
regress by construction." Measured: **349 of 1,910** recovered-rule nodes render *farther* from their own
observed box with the rules than without; **0** render closer; **98,613px** of introduced width error; worst
single node p000007 `n000328`, observed 1344px → **805px** (a flex box inside a grid, hit by the *full-width*
branch, not the percentage branch). The truth-sanity gate at `:152-160` only compares probe-vs-observation
**before** emitting; nothing anywhere re-renders an emitted rule.

**But deleting the module is a regression, and this correction matters.** A five-state ablation of the emitted
block (with / drop-percentage-only / drop-full-width-only / drop-both / delete-all), scored against the
historical layout probe across 6,853 nodes on 8 pages:

| State | @1920 meanWidthErr | within-2px | meanXErr | visibility mismatches @1024 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| as-built | 17.87 | 91.8% | 4.16 | 444 |
| drop 52 percentage rules only | **1.82** | **97.1%** | **1.80** | 444 |
| drop 1,106 full-width rules | 7.03 | 95.9% | 237.87 | 444 |
| delete emit path entirely | 5.65 | 96.3% | 237.87 | **1153** |

Deleting the emit path puts a **240.00px median horizontal offset on all 8 pages at 1920** and multiplies
responsive visibility errors **2.6×** at 1024 (69 → 576 on /pricing alone). Dropping only the 52
percentage-width rules reproduces the entire /changelog gain of a full deletion **to four decimal places** and
is simultaneously the best state off-viewport on every axis. **The fix is one predicate, not a ~500-line
deletion.** The 42 centred-max-width and 710 responsive-hidden rules are pixel-neutral at 1440 on every route
and load-bearing elsewhere.

## 13. Paint / SVG Findings

**Major in meaning, minor in pixels — and the reported metric ranks it near-last while it destroys the most
information-dense surface on the site.**

- **SVG interior paint is never observed.** The DOM walk `return`s at `<svg>` (`collect-dom.ts:665-671`);
  `fill`/`stroke` are in no whitelist; the QA walk returns at `<svg>` too (`capture-page.ts:278`); the template

  initial value is **black** on a dark ground; `stroke`'s is **none**, which deletes artwork outright.
  Consequence, read directly off `band-02-cr76.png`: ~252 check/cross glyphs in the /pricing plan table go from
  solid white ticks to near-invisible dark discs, **13.64:1 → 1.15:1** contrast, across 5 routes and 9 audited

- **`mask-composite` is unobserved and covers a large area — but its pixel share does not survive a
  perceptible threshold.** `STYLE_WHITELIST` captures mask layers (`types.ts:361-369`) but not the

  pixel half is. Wave 2 reported the three masked rects on / desktop as 96/96/95% changed and together

  | | rect 1 <br>(−72,2859,1478,626) | rect 2 <br>(48,4094,1344,618) | rect 3 <br>(−9,7602,1458,598) | all three |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | changed `@1` | 87.3% | 84.4% | 80.0% | **66.3%** of the route's changed pixels |

  | mean max-channel Δ | 8.02 | 5.62 | 7.03 | rect area = **16.4%** of the frame |

  Read the bottom two rows together: at `@16` the three rects hold **16.7%** of the changed pixels over
  **16.4%** of the area — no concentration whatsoever. The mask-composite loss is a real and structurally
  proven defect that washes a large surface at roughly **5–8/255** amplitude, which is precisely the RC-30

## 17. Why Existing QA Passed

The artifact was recorded `status: "complete"` on 14 of 16 pairs and shipped. Six mechanisms explain it, all
verified in code.

1. **Nothing in the pipeline gates on pixels.** `src/reconstruction-qa/screenshot-diff.ts:29` states verbatim:
   *"No PASS score (item 31). Nothing here returns a verdict."* `changedPixelRatio` appears **only** inside
   `src/reconstruction-qa/` (types, qa-page, summarize, screenshot-diff) — **zero** consumers in
   `src/release/`, `src/production/` or `src/recon-template/` gating.
2. **The template gate has no pixel term.** `src/recon-template/parity-qa.ts:364-370`:
   `pass = contentEqual && structureEqual && docHeightDelta <= LIMIT && geometry.p95 <= LIMIT &&
   introducedJsErrors.length === 0 && template.hydrationErrors.length === 0`. The run-level pass at `:821-826`
   adds only interaction equivalence and mutation checks.
3. **The route check is HTTP-200-only** — the body is fetched and discarded, so a 200 with a blank body counts
   as rendered. `baseline-summary.json`: `routeCheck {checked: 8, rendered: 8, failures: []}` sitting beside
   `screenshotChangedRatioMedian: 0.1679`.
4. **Geometry parity is believed without ever being interrogated — and it is only partly manufactured.** The
   generator bakes 5,740 `width:<px>` and **5,779** `height:<px>` declarations onto the nodes, so no paint or
   text-metric error can move a box: it overprints, overflows, or leaves a hole inside a frozen one. The
   accepted run therefore reports `geometryMedianOfMedians 0.01`, `geometryMedianOfP95 0.085`,
   `documentHeightDeltaMedian 0.28` while 2.35% to 49.53% of pixels differ `@1`.
   **The stronger form of this claim is refuted, and this report does not assert it.** Wave 1 argued "geometry
   parity is an artifact of baked pixel values, therefore the reconstruction only *looks* right";
   `28.5-root-causes.json → claimsCorrectedByVerification[2]` records that as **REFUTED (2 of 2 lenses)**, with
   counter-measurements this report accepts: stripping all 5,740 baked widths changes document height by
   **0.00%** on every desktop route; stripping all 5,779 baked heights leaves 6/16 pairs within 2px and 9/16
   within 0.3%; and un-baking makes **paint worse on 4/4 routes tested**, up to **13.3×** on /integrations
   (0.0235 → 0.3131 `@1`), because the observer preserves not one source CSS rule — so the counterfactual to
   baking is collapse, not graceful reflow. Height parity is therefore substantially **earned** by faithfully
   reproduced box-model flow. Two narrower things survive, and they are all this section needs: baking a
   **used** value re-encodes it as an **authored** value and silently changes box-model semantics (RC-15,
   RC-17, RC-18); and — independently of how the parity arises — the geometry metric cannot see paint at all,
   which is why it was trusted. The "geometry parity is not sufficient" conclusion rests on the second of
   those, not on the refuted first.
5. **Style comparison is near-vacuous by construction.** `styleComparedProperties 1,381,062` with
   `styleMismatchedProperties 629` — and `styleMismatchByProperty` is `{width 478, grid-template-columns 73,
   grid-template-rows 22, max-width 11, min-height 32, margin-left 6, margin-right 6, line-height 1}`. Every
   property in this report's top six is **absent from that list because it is absent from the vocabulary**.
6. **Detection terminates instead of escalating.** 460 of 680 emitted diffs carry
   `autoFixEligibility: 'not-eligible-no-correction-type'` and `recommendation: 'none'`. The /pricing logo
   collapse is recorded verbatim (`qd000623` dw −539 dx +269.5; `qd000651` width 1344 → 805, both
   `upstreamStage: 'reconstruction'`) and nothing acts on it. Being seen is not being gated.

## 21. Hybrid Experiment

**Variant C — variant B plus `data-wr-node` identity on every element plus one last-linked override
stylesheet.**

- **Pixel cost of the editability layer: exactly zero.** C scores identically to B-plus to four decimal places
  on all six audited pairs — **all `@1`, all vs CURRENT_SOURCE in-session**, in the order
  **desktop 1440 / wide 1920 / mobile 390**: /security **0.0025 / 0.0018 / 0.0021**, /pricing
  **0.0003 / 0.0002 / 0.0019**. The matching live-vs-live session floors are 0.0019 / 0.0013 / 0.0017 and
  0.0000 / 0.0000 / 0.0008, so C sits at 1.3–1.4× the floor on /security and **at** the floor on /pricing.
  Not re-scored at `@16`.
- **Capability, tested rather than assumed:** **719 of 720** override attempts through `[data-wr-node]` in a
  last-linked stylesheet actually changed computed style (6 properties × 120 sampled visible elements ×
  2 routes). The single loss was a `LABEL` background on /pricing lost to source specificity.
- **Reflow works where the current pipeline's does not:** a text swap grew the `h1` 136 → 271px (2 → 4 lines)
  at 1440 and 88 → 220px (2 → 5 lines) at 390, page 3231 → 3366 and 4783 → 4915, 0 overlaps,
  `text-wrap: balance` honoured.
- **The C0 ablation** (CSSOM harvest disabled) lands at **0.0474–0.1273 `@1`** across those same six pairs
  vs CURRENT_SOURCE — best case /security wide 1920, worst case /security mobile 390 — with `scrollHeight`
  off by up to +281px and `commonAreaRatio` down to 0.9445. That is 20×–300× worse: the harvest step is
  mandatory, not optional. Not re-scored at `@16`.

**Two failure modes, and both are untested by anyone.**

1. A text replacement on /pricing was **silently clipped** by source CSS with no reflow at all.
2. **Node-identity stability across re-capture is unvalidated.** The live DOM shed **188–197 elements** between
   two captures of the same route within one hour (/security 2,123 → 1,926; /pricing 3,434 → 3,246). Any
   positional identity scheme will re-key operator edits onto different elements on the next harvest.

**Verdict on the question as asked:** the hybrid does **not** improve fidelity over preservation — it is the
same artifact plus an inert attribute. It improves **authorability** over preservation at zero pixel cost, and
it inherits **every one** of preservation's generalisation failures, plus the same licensing exposure
(it redistributes Inter Variable, Berkeley Mono and Tiempos verbatim), plus the loss of regenerability, plus
the loss of 9,929 slot bindings and the semantic model.

## 25. Things We Should NOT Build

Named specifically because the evidence rules them out — including the ones this investigation was itself
tempted by.

1. **A layout engine.** Nothing in the corpus needs one. The top four defects are two allowlist properties, one
   missing at-rule channel, and one arithmetic predicate.
2. **AI CSS repair.** No experiment produced evidence that any inference beyond exact measurement is required.
3. **More layout inference.** The module is already net-negative at its own truth viewport (349 nodes worse,
   0 better). Fix the denominator; do not extend the module.
4. **…but equally, do NOT delete `layout-inference`.** This was the investigation's own strongest temptation —
   an experiment measured deletion as an improvement at 1440 and proposed it as a ~500-line simplification.
   Off-viewport it is a severe regression: **240.00px median horizontal offset on all 8 pages at 1920** and
   **2.6× more responsive visibility errors at 1024** (69 → 576 on /pricing). The 42 centred-max-width and 710
   responsive-hidden rules are pixel-neutral at 1440 on every route and load-bearing everywhere else.
5. **A wholesale switch to preserved stylesheets or the hybrid.** Best of six other sites is 33× worse than
   linear.app; the naive form is worse than what we ship today; the good numbers depend on a
   styled-components CSSOM quirk that occurred on none of the six; and it discards regenerability plus 9,929
   slot bindings, named nodes, theme tokens and content-injection accounting.
6. **A single-viewport frozen full-computed artifact (variant D).** 13×/53× worse at 1920; at 390 it is the
   wrong page (commonArea 0.2708, 203 wrong `display:none`); an `h1` text swap moves it **0px** while the
   source grows 203px; 31.6MB for one route at one viewport.
7. **A naive DOM-snapshot archiver with no CSSOM harvest.** Measurably worse than today **and it fails
   silently**.
8. **Per-viewport duplicate DOM trees.** Serving ONE desktop-captured (1440) DOM at 1920 and at 390 scored
   identically to per-viewport snapshots on /security — **wide 1920: 0.0018 vs 0.0018; mobile 390: 0.0021 vs
   0.0022**, both `@1` vs CURRENT_SOURCE — measured three times independently. The dual
   tree is the direct cause of the doubled pseudo counts (122 vs 61), doubled `<svg>` counts (682 vs 338) and
   the whole variant-collision / fragment-id-ambiguity class.
9. **A zero-diff pixel gate.** The residual of every variant — including the live source against itself — is a
   marquee at a different phase: **0.0013–0.0019 `@1` live-vs-live on /security** across wide 1920 / mobile 390
   / desktop 1440 in one session (§16). A gate at 0 can never pass. Any gate needs an
   animation-freeze step, `commonAreaRatio`, per-node geometry, and a perceptible threshold — **and must land
   after fixes 1–5 or it will simply fail everything.**
10. **A per-shape inlined SVG paint fix** (the F3 simulation's second half). It hard-codes paint onto 58–116
    individual shapes where a content slot should be, breaking the template layer. The correct form is a walk
    into the shared style table.
11. **Per-subtree SVG fragment-id scoping.** As simulated it left 12 and 8 references still ambiguous because
    they sit outside any `[data-wr-viewport]` subtree. If built, build it document-wide.
12. **A single global `:root` custom-property snapshot.** Already lossy on the measured site:
    `--homepage-outer-padding` is redefined at three breakpoints and `--border-hairline` under a
    device-pixel-ratio query. Capture per viewport or reproduce `layout-inference`'s exact class of bug.
13. **Any font pipeline that ships before a licensing gate.** Inter Variable, Berkeley Mono and Tiempos
    Headline are third-party licensed faces already classified `license-needs-review` at
    `src/assets/fonts.ts:200-203`.
14. **Continued reliance on `document.fonts.check()` as a health signal.** It returned **true** with 0 faces
    loaded and with 4. It is worthless as a gate and currently hides the rank-1 defect family.
15. **Per-site selector patching of any kind**, including linear.app-specific fixes for the pricing table.

## 26. Recommended Reconstruction Strategy

**Keep observe → IR → regenerate. Make three bounded changes, in this order, because the ordering is causal
and was measured.**

**(A) Widen the observed vocabulary where the CSS initial value is provably the wrong default.**
Add `opacity` and `visibility` to `PSEUDO_STYLE_WHITELIST`; add `-webkit-font-smoothing`, `text-wrap-mode`,
`text-wrap-style` to `STYLE_WHITELIST`; capture `:root` custom properties **per viewport**; and open a font
channel that materialises `@font-face` plus the face files from the network responses the Observer already
sees. Four small allowlist edits plus one bounded (~200–400 line) asset channel. **Size it from the whole ladder,
not from a band chosen after the fact:** faces-plus-smoothing removes **5.8% to 97.4% of `@16` pixels across
the 7 desktop routes measured** (§11), median **74.5%** — four routes at 74.5–97.4% and three far below
(46.6% home, 16.9% /changelog, **5.8%** /plan, where the residual is missing imagery and layout recovery, not
typography). An earlier draft of this report quoted "75–97% on text-dominated routes"; that band excludes
/pricing at 74.5% and is only reachable by naming which routes count as text-dominated after seeing the
result, so it is withdrawn. Adopt the invariant explicitly: **an allowlist may drop a property only when that
property's CSS initial value is a safe default.**

**(B) Fix the two generator logic errors — do not delete the module.** Give `layout-inference` a
containing-block-aware denominator (grid area / flex basis / positioned ancestor padding box, and subtract
border-width), and add the post-emit re-render check its docstring already promises. The second half is what
makes the first half verifiable on the **next** site rather than only on this one.

**(C) Add one observation channel, not a new engine.** Harvest the source's `@media`/`@container` conditions
and the rules under them — in the **same live session** as the cross-origin HTTP bodies, because neither path
alone is sufficient — and store them **alongside** the per-node tokens, letting them govern widths the Observer
never visited while per-node tokens govern the observed widths. This is the only thing in the audit that fixes
responsiveness, it is additive to the IR, and it keeps slots, regenerability and the semantic layer.

**State the risk in (C) plainly.** Nobody has demonstrated that source `@media` rules can coexist with 5,740
baked `width:<px>` and 5,779 baked `height:<px>` declarations without the tokens winning at every width. If
they cannot, this stops being an added channel and becomes a redesign. **Take the cheap partial mitigations
first:** stop discarding the 768px probe row at `layout-inference.ts:131`, and emit the recovered tier for the
mobile subtree too.

**Adopt these mechanism findings from the rejected architectures**, which are correct regardless of which
architecture wins: harvest CSSOM and HTTP bodies in one session; `adoptedStyleSheets` is invisible to **both**
`outerHTML` and `document.styleSheets` and must be read explicitly; and a stylesheet whose `textContent` is
empty but whose `cssRules` is non-empty is a real and generic pattern (styled-components v6, Emotion speedy
mode, JSS, Stitches, Goober).

## 28. Final Verdict

RAW RECONSTRUCTION NEEDS TARGETED FIDELITY HARDENING

**Justification.** Three of the four permitted verdicts are eliminated by artifact evidence.

*"Sound — problem is downstream"* is dead. The recon-template boundary is MD5-identical on 8/8 route/viewport
pairs with `reconstruction-data` byte-identical, and an independent sweep reproduced that artifact's document
heights exactly (/ @1440 = 10898, / @390 = 6382, /pricing @1440 = 6348, /pricing @390 = 7712). Raw
reconstruction carries the entire delta — and it is decisively wrong: 17 of 19 pairs obviously-different, one
unusable, one minor, with a pricing comparison table whose ~252 glyphs are near-invisible dark discs under a
spurious painted bar on every row, and a changelog entry whose date column is halved into a three-line stack
with a neighbouring label at one character per line and its video an empty black box.

*"Requires fundamental redesign"* is not supported. Every defect in the top six traces to a named allowlist
entry, one predicate, or one hardcoded string, each located in shipped code. Variant D proves per-node computed
style is **capable** of near-exactness at the capture viewport (0.0008 / 0.0038 `@1`), so the strategy is not
intrinsically broken. What it cannot do is off-viewport, which is one added channel rather than a new engine.

*"Style strategy should be simplified"* is the closest call and is rejected on generalisation. Preserved
stylesheets win overwhelmingly on this site (`@16` 0.0016 / 0.0002 against the accepted artifact's 0.0381 /
0.0295, with one desktop capture reproducing live `scrollHeight` exactly at 9/9 widths) — but the same pipeline
on six other sites lands at `@16` 0.0598–0.2673 against per-site floors of 0.0000–0.0041, walled off by shadow
DOM and JS canvas paint; the naive form is **worse** than what we ship today (0.0630 vs 0.0460 `@1` on
/security); the good numbers depend on a styled-components CSSOM quirk that appeared on none of those six; and
it discards 9,929 slot bindings, named nodes, theme tokens, content-injection accounting and regenerability,
which is the product. Adopt its mechanism findings, not its architecture.

That leaves targeted hardening, and the ladder supports it at the size claimed: four allowlist lines plus one
denominator predicate move /pricing 0.3831 → 0.0104 and /security 0.0460 → 0.0037 `@1`, with two of the rungs
at two lines each.

**The honest counter-argument, in five parts, none of which is resolved by this audit.**

1. **The dominant perceptible fix is licensing-blocked.** Strip fonts out of the ladder and what remains is far
   less impressive: the pseudo fix is worth ~0 at `@16` on /pricing (fixes 2,018 px, regresses 3,054), custom
   properties are 0.4–1.1% `@1`, the fragment-id fix is worth **exactly 0 pixels** on three routes at four
   thresholds. The rung that carries the perceptible gap on both audited routes — 82.9% of /security standalone,
   74.9% of /pricing's `@16` improvement — is shipping licensed WOFF2 files that Task 22 already classified
   `license-needs-review`. If licensing blocks that on a real pilot, the #1 fix does not ship and the honest
   achievable ladder is a few percent of perceptible pixels.
2. **The responsive fix is the largest piece of unbuilt work in the audit and nobody has built it in the
   recommended form.** Source `@media` rules were only ever measured inside a *different artifact shape* where
   they govern a verbatim source DOM. Their composition with 5,740 baked `width:<px>` and 5,779 baked
   `height:<px>` declarations is unproven. That composition may require dropping the per-node exact tier at
   unobserved widths — which is closer to a redesign than to hardening. Its measured benefit is honestly
   **"unmeasured in the recommended form"**, and it is the fix the verdict most depends on. It is classified
   REPLACE SUBSYSTEM inside a hardening verdict rather than pretended to be small.
3. **The rejected challenger is measured; the incumbent is not.** Nobody re-ran the current pipeline on those
   six other sites. It is entirely possible the current architecture scores worse than 0.2673 `@16` on lit.dev.
   This is a measured challenger against an unmeasured incumbent on the generalisation axis, and the two worst
   preservation failures (lit.dev 0.2593, MDN 0.2673) are shadow-DOM collapse that a shadow-root-aware
   harvester could plausibly fix.
4. **The measurement substrate is softer than four-decimal precision suggests.** Three parties produced two
   different answers for /changelog desktop (0.1491 vs 0.1757 `@1`), and a wave-3 independent capture agreed
   with wave 1, not with the recheck that had declared wave 1 wrong. Within-harness determinism is not in
   doubt; cross-harness determinism on that route is. Every route-level ranking here carries more uncertainty
   than its decimals imply, which is why the layout-inference finding rests on harness-independent evidence
   (width p90 = p95 = 320.73 = 624 − 303.27 over 571 mismatched nodes) rather than on any disputed pixel number.
5. **"Targeted hardening" may be the wrong shape for a root cause that is architectural by construction.**
   17 of 31 defects are one failure mode — a closed hand-curated allowlist silently substituting the CSS
   initial value. Each fix is two lines, but the process generating the defects is unbounded: `text-wrap` was
   found by an experimenter staring at a residual crop, not by any systematic method, and it halved what was
   left. There is no evidence the allowlist has converged, and a structural argument that it cannot — because
   the QA's comparison vocabulary is defined **as** the allowlist (`capture-page.ts:46`), the pipeline is
   constitutionally unable to discover what it is missing. Someone could fairly argue that a design whose
   defect rate is bounded only by how long people stare at crops needs replacing, and that this verdict merely
   schedules the next five two-line fixes. **Fix #9 — decoupling the QA vocabulary and adding a perceptible
   channel — is the only item on the list that attacks that argument rather than deferring it, which is why it
   is in wave 1 and not later.**

**What this audit could not measure at all:** interactive after-states (hover, focus, sticky, menu-open,
accordion, the pricing toggle) against any reference; any route or viewport outside the 8 routes ×
{390, 1440} profiles plus a 1920 wide audit and the clone-only width sweeps; dark mode, which is structurally
unobservable under the hard-pinned observation context; and the second-site behaviour of every fix in §24.
